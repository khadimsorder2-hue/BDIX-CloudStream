SkStream Web Player · standalone demo

Pixel-copy of the CloudStream app player. Paste any direct video / .mkv / .m3u8 URL (or a site URL to route through the local proxy), then press Play. This module is fully standalone — player.css + player.js only.

Gestures & shortcuts

  • Double-tap left / right — ±10s · double-tap center — play / pause
  • Single tap — controls visible for 15s (any touch resets the timer; auto-hide only after 15s with no touch) · long-press — 2x speed
  • Source chip in the top bar switches server instantly · Fit / Crop / Stretch button on the bottom row
  • space play/pause · ←→ seek · ↑↓ volume · m mute · esc back
  • Skip Intro pill appears in the first 85s (for long videos) · loop, lock, PiP in the ⋮ menu
